Features

Everything the Tribes 3v3 script does

Six core modules, all driven from one Lua file and toggled with a single key bind.

🎯

Aimbot

Configurable FOV, smoothness and bone targeting so your shots land consistently in every 3v3 duel.

👁️

ESP Overlay

Name, health, distance and weapon readouts drawn over each enemy so you always know who is where.

🧱

Wallhack

See enemy silhouettes through walls and terrain to track rotations and hold the right angle before contact.

⚡

Speed Hack

Adjustable movement multiplier with a hotkey toggle, letting you reposition faster without breaking your input.

🛡️

Auto-Block

Automatically times blocks and parries against incoming melee attacks so you win more close-range trades.

🧩

Injector Loader

A lightweight loader that injects the Lua script into the Tribes 3v3 process in one click — no manual setup.
